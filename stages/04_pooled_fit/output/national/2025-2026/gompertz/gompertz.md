# National Gompertz model, 2025 + 2026

Generated by `gompertz_pooled.py`. The two national seasons are stacked. Each row of the data is one athlete-season.

**The model.** Above the threshold u, the share of athlete-seasons at time t or faster is S(t) = ζ · exp(−c·(e^(y/b) − 1)). Here y = u − t is how far t is faster than u, and ζ = N_u / n. The return period is 1/S(t): one athlete-season in N.

**The threshold.** ForwardStop selects u (Anderson–Darling test, α = 0.05, 18 thresholds from the top 20% to the top 0.5%). If it rejects nothing, u is the top 5%. Before the test, each time is spread by ±0.005 s. Times have only hundredths, and the ties otherwise make the test reject everything in a large pool.

**Uncertainty.** 200 bootstrap resamples of complete athletes. One athlete in both seasons stays together. Each resample takes u again at the same quantile level.
